Fashion Design

The fashion design program focuses on building a strong design foundation. Students learn about fabrics, silhouettes, styling, and garment construction. Interior Design

The interior design program helps students understand spaces, layouts, and materials. Students learn how to plan functional interiors while keeping aesthetics in mind, with practical assignments on real-world design challenges.
