Our Story

A Heritage of Design Excellence & Creative Mastery

Established as a trailblazing design education powerhouse, NIF Global Ghatkopar has earned a distinguished reputation for shaping aspiring creators into successful professionals. We bridge the critical gap between academic theory and high-stakes commercial fashion and interior industries.

Our campus at Sanghvi Square on M.G. Road features state-of-the-art garment construction labs, drafting studios, cutting tables, computerized CAD/CAM workstations, and a rich materials library.

Our Vision

To be India’s premier design powerhouse producing globally recognized, commercially adept fashion and interior creators.

Our Mission

To deliver rigorous practical mentorship, runway exposure at Lakmé Fashion Week, and 100% placement support for every student.
