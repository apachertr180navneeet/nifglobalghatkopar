FAQ’s

How do I apply for scholarships or financial aid?

NIF Global Mumbai offers merit-based scholarships and need-based financial assistance. Submit your academic transcripts, portfolio (if applicable), and a scholarship application form; shortlisted candidates are interviewed by our committee. Flexible EMI plans with 0% interest are also available.

What kind of placements and internships are available?

We maintain strong ties with leading fashion houses, interior design firms, and beauty brands. Past interns have worked on fashion weeks, premium interior projects, and editorial shoots, and our placement team assists with interviews and portfolio presentations.

Can I transfer credits if I’ve already studied design elsewhere?

Yes. Submit your previous transcripts and detailed syllabi to our admissions office. A committee reviews your coursework and transfers credits where it aligns with our curriculum.

What support services are available for students after graduation?

Alumni receive career counseling, placement assistance, networking opportunities, an alumni portal with job listings, meetups, webinars and masterclasses.

What are the eligibility criteria for admission?

Candidates must have completed 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognized board. For diploma and certificate courses, work experience or portfolios are considered; the foundation course is open to all creative aspirants.
