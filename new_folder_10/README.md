## 🔤 Typography

- **Headings & Body Font**: `'Poppins', sans-serif`
- **Display Accent Font**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif`
- **Font Weights Used**:
  - `300` (Light body text)
  - `400` (Regular)
  - `500` (Medium navigation & labels)
  - `600` (Semi-bold headings & buttons)
  - `700` & `800` (Bold display numbers & hero titles)
