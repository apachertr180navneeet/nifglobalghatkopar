Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about admissions, fees, scholarships, or placements? Here are answers.

How do I apply for scholarships or financial aid at NIF Global Ghatkopar? +
NIF Global Ghatkopar offers merit-based scholarships and need-based financial assistance. Submit your 10th/12th academic transcripts, creative portfolio (if applicable), and scholarship form. Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview.
What is the placement assistance process? +
Our dedicated placement cell conducts portfolio reviews, mock interviews, and resumes workshops. Graduating students are directly connected to top fashion brands, celebrity stylists, interior design firms, and architectural studios.
What are the eligibility criteria for Fashion and Interior Design courses? +
Candidates who have passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) with an interest in design and creativity are eligible for diploma and degree programs.
