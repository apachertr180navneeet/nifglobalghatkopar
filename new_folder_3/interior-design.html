Reasons Why You Should Choose Interior Design?

  • Explosive Real Estate Growth: Mumbai's redevelopment and luxury residential expansion have skyrocketed demand for qualified interior architects.
  • High-Value Remuneration: Interior designers command project commissions of 10%–15% of total renovation budgets, providing substantial earning potential.
  • Blend of Art & Engineering: Harmonize aesthetics, lighting physics, acoustic optimization, and psychological color harmony.
  • Flexible Freelancing & Studio Ownership: Start handling independent residential turnkey contracts within 18 months of study.

Mentorship by Gauri Khan & Twinkle Khanna

  • Gauri Khan Masterclasses: Understand luxury residential styling, European sourcing trips, and custom furniture curation.
  • The White Window Philosophy: Learn organic styling, coastal aesthetics, and lifestyle retail curation from Twinkle Khanna.
  • Site Visits & Material Labs: Regular guided field visits to marble yards, modular kitchen factories, and live construction sites in Mumbai.
  • 100% Placement Assistance: Direct internships with renowned interior firms, real estate developers, and design consultancies.
