About NIF Global Ghatkopar

Empowering creative aspirants across Mumbai with hands-on learning, world-class mentors, and international runways.

WHO WE ARE

Shaping Creative Visionaries in Mumbai

At NIF Global Ghatkopar, we believe creativity isn’t just taught — it’s cultivated through vision, discipline, and hands-on experience. Founded with a mission to transform creative minds into accomplished professionals, our institute stands as a beacon of excellence in fashion and interior design education.

With deep roots in Mumbai’s vibrant design community, we provide a dynamic learning environment where innovation meets real-world relevance. Whether you’re looking to explore the world of fashion or refine your interior design aesthetic, NIF Global offers a nurturing platform that encourages bold ideas, creative risk-taking, and technical expertise.

Affiliated with the London School of Trends, our students receive international certifications and regularly participate in global runways including New York Fashion Week, London Fashion Week, Dubai, and Lakmé Fashion Week.
