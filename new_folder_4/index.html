Degree & Diploma

Fashion Design

The fashion design program focuses on building a strong design foundation. Students learn about fabrics, silhouettes, styling, and garment construction. Creative thinking is developed through regular design work and hands-on projects. The aim is to help students understand how fashion works as a profession, not just as an art form.

Degree & Diploma

Interior Design

The interior design program helps students understand spaces, layouts, and materials. Students learn how to plan functional interiors while keeping aesthetics in mind. Practical assignments are used to explain real world design challenges. This helps students gain the skills needed to design residential and commercial environments effectively.

BBA Degree

Design Management Program

The design management program combines creative thinking with business skills. Students study market trends, brand building, merchandising, and project planning. This program is for those who want to work in leadership or strategy roles within the fashion and design sectors, bridging the gap between design concepts and business outcomes.

NIF Global Mumbai offers merit-based scholarships and need-based financial assistance. To apply, submit your academic transcripts and creative portfolio during the admission process. Our scholarship jury evaluates creative potential, academic performance, and personal interviews. Flexible zero-cost EMI installment options are also available.

For undergraduate degree and diploma programs, candidates must have completed 10+2 from any recognized board (Arts, Commerce, or Science). No prior drawing or CAD experience is required as our foundation modules start completely from the basics.

Students have exclusive opportunities to showcase their design collections at New York Fashion Week, London Fashion Week, Dubai, and Lakmé Fashion Week. In partnership with the London School of Trends (LST), students can also participate in residential summer styling programs in London.
