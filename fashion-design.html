Curriculum Roadmap

Structured Learning Journey

From fundamental design principles to runway collection presentation.

1st Year • Foundation Program

Design Foundations & Illustration

Build strong fundamental drawing skills, understand color theory, textile anatomy, and basic pattern drafting.

  • Introduction to Fashion & Design History
  • Fashion Illustration & Model Drawing
  • Textile Science, Weaving & Fiber Study
  • Basic Pattern Making & Garment Construction
  • Hand Embroidery & Surface Ornamentation
Certificate / Foundation Diploma
2nd Year • Advance Program

Advanced Draping & Digital CAD

Explore digital fashion styling, computerized pattern making, couture draping, and commercial production techniques.

  • Advanced Pattern Drafting & Grading
  • Draping on Dress Forms & Corsetry
  • Computer-Aided Design (Photoshop & Illustrator)
  • Traditional Indian Textiles & Costumes
  • Garment Production & Quality Control
Advanced Diploma Certification
3rd Year • Degree & Specialization

Runway Collection & Brand Launch

Produce an original fashion collection, participate in runway exhibitions, and master fashion merchandising & marketing.

  • Graduating Runway Collection Presentation
  • Fashion Forecasting & Trend Research
  • Luxury Brand Marketing & Merchandising
  • Fashion Styling & Editorial Photography
  • 100% Industry Internship & Placement
