FAQ'S

What is the minimum qualification required to enroll in a fashion design course?

To enroll in our fashion design programs, applicants must have completed their 10th or 12th grade from a recognized board. For advanced or specialization programs, prior experience or a portfolio may enhance your application but is not mandatory.

Are the courses suitable for beginners with no prior design experience?

Absolutely. Our one-year foundation program is designed specifically for beginners. It introduces students to core design principles, drawing techniques, fabric understanding, and basic garment construction—making it ideal for those starting from scratch.

What career options can I pursue after completing the course?

Upon completing your fashion design course, you can explore careers such as fashion designer, stylist, fashion illustrator, textile designer, merchandiser, or even launch your own label. Our curriculum also prepares you for roles in fashion marketing, research, and entrepreneurship.

Do you provide internships or placement support?

Yes. We offer 100% placement assistance through partnerships with top fashion houses and design firms. Our students also participate in internships and live projects during the course, gaining valuable real-world exposure and networking opportunities.

Can I start my own fashion business after completing the course?

Definitely. Our programs include modules on fashion business management, branding, and entrepreneurship. Many of our graduates have gone on to start successful clothing lines or online fashion ventures with the skills and confidence they gained here.
