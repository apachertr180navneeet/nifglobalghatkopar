Architecture & Design

Transform Your Passion into a Fulfilling Architectural Career

At NIF Global Ghatkopar, interior design goes far beyond simple decoration — it is the science of human ergonomics, light, materials, and sustainable architecture. Our programs equip students to craft bespoke residential havens, luxury hospitality venues, and modern commercial offices.

With hands-on software training in AutoCAD, 3ds Max, V-Ray, SketchUp, and Revit, coupled with live construction site visits and vendor showroom tours across Mumbai, our graduates build remarkable commercial portfolios.
