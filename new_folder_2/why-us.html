KEY HIGHLIGHTS

Why Choose NIF Global Mumbai – Key Highlights

A curriculum built with direct inputs from India's celebrated designers and international institutions.

FASHION WEEKS

At NIF Global Ghatkopar, students don’t just learn—they live the fashion experience. We offer the rare opportunity to participate in world-renowned fashion weeks, including Lakmé Fashion Week, London Fashion Week, New York Fashion Week, and Dubai Fashion Week. These events provide a global platform for our students to showcase their designs, interact with leading designers, and understand international fashion markets.

ONLINE COURSES

In collaboration with the London School of Trends (LST), we offer exclusive online learning opportunities mentored by fashion icon Manish Malhotra. These programs blend global insights with accessible formats, making it possible for students to learn from anywhere while receiving world-class instruction and international credentials.

GOVT. RECOGNISED

We offer government-recognized 3-year and 2-year specialization courses in Fashion and Interior Design, ensuring that your education is both credible and career-ready. These programs are thoughtfully designed to meet evolving industry standards while maintaining strong academic rigor and recognized university degrees.

100% PLACEMENT ASST.

Education is only the beginning. That’s why NIF Global provides 100% placement assistance through strategic collaborations with top brands and design studios. Support includes interview prep, portfolio development, and real-time live projects. Students often get placed even before graduation.

CENTRALLY LOCATED

Conveniently situated in the heart of Ghatkopar at Sanghvi Square, the institute is easily accessible by both Metro Rail and Local trains. This central location makes it effortless for visiting celebrity mentors, guest faculty, and students from across Mumbai to commute.

WORLD CLASS INFRA

NIF Global Ghatkopar boasts a Wi-Fi-enabled campus with modern infrastructure. Advanced classrooms and studios are equipped with cutting-edge tools, CAD suites, audio-visual systems, pattern-making tables, and photography setups curated to inspire hands-on design readiness.
