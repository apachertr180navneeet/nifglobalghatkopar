Our Heritage

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Innovation

Established as a trailblazing design powerhouse, NIF Global Ghatkopar has earned distinguished prestige for transforming creative beginners into runway-ready couturiers and high-demand interior architects.

Our campus at Sanghvi Square on M.G. Road boasts advanced garment construction laboratories, industrial cutting and draping suites, computerized CAD/CAM drafting workstations, and an extensive physical materials library.

Our Vision

To remain India's premier design destination producing globally recognized, commercially adept creative leaders.

Our Mission

To deliver rigorous studio mentorship, international runway exposure, and 100% placement support for every aspiring designer.
