About NIF Global Ghatkopar

A Creative Sanctuary Situated in the Heart of Mumbai

Located just seconds from Ghatkopar Metro & Railway Station at Sanghvi Square, NIF Global Ghatkopar {Platinum Center} bridges classic craftsmanship with 21st-century digital innovation.

How Learning Works at NIF Ghatkopar

A progressive 4-step practical learning methodology designed to bridge classroom creativity with industry-ready execution.

01

Conceptual Clarity

Design fundamentals, creative sketching, moodboards, color psychology, and rigorous textile & spatial material research.

02

Hands-On Studio Work

Transforming 2D sketches into tangible garments and 3D architectural spaces in sewing suites and CAD computer drafting labs.

03

Celebrity Masterclasses

Direct portfolio reviews and masterclasses from iconic legends Manish Malhotra, Gauri Khan, and Ashley Rebello.

04

Runway & 100% Placement

Collection launches at Lakmé Fashion Week and campus recruitment with India’s leading luxury fashion houses and design studios.
