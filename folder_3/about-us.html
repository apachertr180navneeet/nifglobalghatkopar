About NIF Global Ghatkopar

Empowering creative aspirants across Mumbai with hands-on learning, world-class mentors, and international runways.

WHO WE ARE

Shaping Creative Visionaries in Mumbai

At NIF Global Ghatkopar, we believe creativity isn’t just taught — it’s cultivated through disciplined practice, industry exposure, and creative risk-taking. Located conveniently at Sanghvi Square adjacent to Ghatkopar Metro Station, our campus serves as a vibrant design incubator.

Affiliated with the London School of Trends and leading academic universities, our students showcase their capstone designs at New York Fashion Week, London Fashion Week, and Lakmé Fashion Week.
